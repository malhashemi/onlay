06

A signal, placed.

The mark is not decoration — it is the product model drawn. Two rings, two points, and the human act between them.

1 2 3 4

Read it the way the product works:

  1. 1
    The boundary ring

    onlay’s layer around the page. It breaks open in exactly one place — where an agent is waiting. It opens; it is never breached.

  2. 2
    The loose signal

    An unanchored Ask, holding the break. An agent may raise it; only a human may place it.

  3. 3
    The context ring

    The page’s context, made visible. Where judgment happens.

  4. 4
    The anchored point

    Human judgment, placed — reaching toward the waiting signal. That reach is Anchor, the act that joins the two.
